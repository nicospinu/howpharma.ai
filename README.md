# HowPharma.AI

A landscape of **open-source AI agents** for drug discovery and development, mapped as a periodic table at [howpharma.ai](https://howpharma.ai).

## What the map shows

- **Industry is already in the open.** Roughly one in three agents has a company in the author list (Genentech, Roche, AstraZeneca, Bayer, AbbVie, Recursion and others). This is not a purely academic fringe.
- **Evidence stops early.** 24 of 30 agents are still evaluated on knowledge, tooling, computational discovery, or expert review (E1–E4). Only 6 show experimental, closed-loop, or translational proof (E5–E7). Design and orchestration are ahead of make/test validation.
- **Pipeline coverage skews upstream.** Hit/lead and preclinical design dominate; synthesis, closed-loop wet work, and clinical/translational paths are thin. There is still almost no credible open path from stack → novel drug.
- **Where to engage.** Treat open agents as accelerators for workflow, knowledge interfaces, and in silico design — not as de-risked discovery engines. Prioritise partners and internals that close the loop (assay feedback, lab automation, prospective evaluation) over another chatbot wrapper.

**October 2026** · **30** agents · **61** organisations · **10** with industry involvement · **6** beyond in silico (wet-lab, closed-loop, or translational evidence).
